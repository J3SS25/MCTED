1. PROJECT TITLE AND SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Project Title:
  Municipal Community Tool and Equipment Bank (MCTEB)
  Municipality of Basco, Province of Batanes

System Overview:
  The Municipal Community Tool and Equipment Bank (MCTEB) is a standardized,
  100% TypeScript civic resource, inventory, reservation, and asset-preservation
  web platform developed for the Local Government Unit (LGU) of Basco, Batanes.
  It enables adult residents across the six barangays of Basco (Chanarian,
  Ihubok I [Kayvaluganan], Ihubok II [Kaychanarianan], Kayhuvokan, San Antonio,
  and San Joaquin) to discover, reserve, and borrow shared agricultural
  machinery, fishing gear, carpentry and construction tools, electrical repair
  equipment, power generators, and emergency storm-response implements at zero
  cost.

Core Architectural Pillars:
  1. End-to-End TypeScript Monorepo:
     Managed via pnpm v12.15.0 workspaces across three TypeScript packages:
     - packages/shared   (@mcteb/shared)   : Authoritative Zod schemas, domain
       enums, RBAC permission matrices, Manila timezone date helpers, and image
       content moderation rules.
     - backend           (@mcteb/backend)  : Express 4 REST API enforcing a
       strict 4-tier architecture (routes -> controllers -> services ->
       repositories -> db) plus an isolated /api/v1/admin perimeter.
     - frontend          (@mcteb/frontend) : React 19 + Vite 6 + Tailwind CSS
       Single-Page Application (SPA) using TanStack Query v5 and React Router
       v7, with responsive sidebar/mobile drawer navigation, light/dark themes,
       and interactive Tool Borrowing Receipt export (A4 PDF & .doc).

  2. Resilient Local-First Embedded Storage:
     Built on Node.js (>= 20) native node:sqlite (DatabaseSync) running in
     Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode with strict foreign key enforcement
     (PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON) and a 5,000 ms busy timeout. Eliminates external
     database daemons and C++ native build compilation (node-gyp), while
     supporting atomic multi-row updates (db.atomic()) and online hot backups
     via VACUUM INTO.

  3. Civic Governance & Traceability:
     Provides server-enforced Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across Borrower,
     Municipal Staff, and Administrator roles, minimum 18-year-old borrower
     eligibility checks, immutable security audit logging, and standardized
     digital/printable Tool Borrowing Receipt forms.

2. BACKGROUND OF THE PROJECT AND PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

Background:
  Basco is the capital municipality of Batanes, an archipelagic island province
  in the northernmost Philippines frequently exposed to severe typhoons,
  monsoonal squalls, maritime supply disruptions, and intermittent internet and
  cellular connectivity. Local farmers, fisherfolk, carpenters, households, and
  barangay disaster-response teams regularly require costly equipment (such as
  hand tillers, 3.5kVA generators, water pumps, concrete mixers, circular saws,
  and marine GPS units) for agricultural livelihood, home repair, and post-storm
  recovery.

  To promote equitable access and disaster readiness without imposing rental
  fees on citizens, the Municipality of Basco operates a shared Community Tool
  and Equipment Bank. However, managing shared civic equipment manually or with
  disconnected spreadsheets introduces operational, accountability, and
  infrastructure failures.

Problem Statement:
  1. Manual Record-Keeping and Custody Loss:
     Paper logbooks at the municipal lending desk make it difficult to track
     real-time shelf availability versus active loans, detect scheduling
     conflicts across advance reservations, or enforce custody accountability
     when tools are overdue or damaged.

  2. Uncontrolled Equipment Degradation:
     Without structured pre-release and post-return condition inspections
     coupled with maintenance defect tickets, worn or damaged tools risk being
     re-issued to subsequent borrowers, creating safety hazards and shortening
     equipment lifespan.

  3. Connectivity and Infrastructure Constraints:
     Cloud-hosted platforms or systems requiring external database servers
     (PostgreSQL/MySQL) fail during typhoon-induced WAN outages and create
     installation and maintenance barriers on municipal office workstations.

  4. Policy Verification and Audit Gaps:
     Manual workflows cannot reliably verify borrower eligibility (e.g., legal
     age >= 18 for custody agreements and Basco barangay residency), prevent
     unauthorized catalog tampering, or preserve an immutable security audit
     trail of administrative and desk actions.

3. PROJECT OBJECTIVES
================================================================================

General Objective:
  To design, implement, and verify a resilient, local-first, role-based
  Municipal Community Tool and Equipment Bank (MCTEB) web platform for the
  Municipality of Basco, Batanes that digitizes equipment cataloging, advance
  reservations, physical handover and return inspections, borrowing receipts,
  maintenance tracking, and municipal governance.

Specific Objectives:
  1. Centralize Equipment Cataloging & Stock Visibility:
     Provide a searchable, filterable equipment registry across seven municipal
     categories (Agriculture, Fishing, Carpentry, Construction, Electrical,
     Power Equipment, and Emergency Response) showing real-time total versus
     available quantities, serial numbers, specifications, and moderated galleries
     of up to 5 images per tool.

  2. Digitize the Reservation & Rental Lifecycle Atomically:
     Support advance resident booking requests with date-range conflict checks,
     municipal staff approval/rejection queues, desk handover release, and
     post-return inspection—using atomic SQLite transactions (db.atomic()) so
     inventory counts, rental statuses, reservation completion, notifications,
     and audit logs remain strictly consistent.

  3. Standardize Borrowing Custody via Tool Borrowing Receipts:
     Provide an interactive 8-section Tool Borrowing Receipt Form pre-populated
     from loan or approved reservation records, supporting digital editing,
     in-browser printing, and client-side generation of A4 PDF (%PDF-1.4) and
     Microsoft Word (.doc) soft copies containing mandatory liability warnings.

  4. Automate Equipment Maintenance & Quarantine:
     Enable maintenance defect reporting (Minor, Moderate, Severe, Critical)
     and return-inspection damage flags that automatically remove compromised
     tools from borrowable stock until repaired and certified back to service.

  5. Enforce Municipal Security, Privacy, and Governance:
     Implement scrypt password hashing, timing-safe comparison, brute-force
     account lockouts, server-enforced RBAC with IDOR protection, administrative
     self-lockout safeguards, surname-masked public community feedback, and an
     immutable audit log.

  6. Deliver Zero-Dependency Local Resilience:
     Operate natively on Node.js >= 20 built-in node:sqlite in WAL mode with
     one-command automated setup, idempotent migrations/seeding, and online hot
     backups (VACUUM INTO).

4. SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

Project Scope:
  - Geographic & Policy Scope:
    Designed specifically for the Municipality of Basco, Province of Batanes,
    supporting its six official barangays (Chanarian, Ihubok I [Kayvaluganan],
    Ihubok II [Kaychanarianan], Kayhuvokan, San Antonio, San Joaquin) and
    enforcing minimum age >= 18 for borrower registration.
  - User Roles Covered:
    * Guest (unauthenticated catalog and public feedback browsing)
    * Borrower (resident account registration, catalog browsing, advance
      reservations, active loan/receipt viewing, notifications, 3-axis feedback)
    * Municipal Staff (catalog/inventory CRUD, reservation review queue, rental
      desk handover/return inspection, borrowing receipts, maintenance board,
      audit view)
    * Administrator (full system oversight, isolated /api/v1/admin dashboard and
      health diagnostics, user role/status management, feedback moderation, and
      immutable audit logs)
  - Functional Modules Implemented:
    9 domain modules (Authentication, Users, Tools/Catalog, Reservations, Rentals
    & Receipts, Maintenance, Feedback, Notifications, Audit Logs) plus the
    isolated Admin perimeter.
  - Operational Tooling:
    Cross-platform setup scripts (setup.bat, setup.ps1, setup.sh), database
    init/migrate/seed/reset-demo/hot-backup commands, and automated Vitest
    test suites across all three workspace packages.

Project Limitations:
  1. In-App Notifications Only (No Live SMS Gateway):
     Reservation approvals, handover confirmations, and return inspection alerts
     are currently delivered exclusively through the in-app notification center
     (/api/v1/notifications). External cellular SMS dispatch (e.g., Semaphore or
     Twilio) is deferred to Phase 3 due to intermittent cellular coverage and
     external gateway subscription requirements.

  2. Single-Node SQLite Write Concurrency:
     SQLite enforces a single-writer lock. While WAL mode enables concurrent
     reads alongside writes and a 5,000 ms busy_timeout queues brief write
     contention, MCTEB targets a single municipal server (~9,000 residents and
     ~10 concurrent desk staff). Scaling to a multi-server province-wide
     deployment across all Batanes municipalities would require swapping the
     repository SQL layer to PostgreSQL.

  3. Free Civic Lending Model (No Financial/Payment Gateway):
     By municipal policy, tool borrowing is free of rental fees or deposits. All
     legacy fee/deposit/penalty/payment tables and columns were explicitly purged
     in schema migrations (backend/db/migrate.ts); online payment gateways are
     out of scope.

  4. Local Image Storage & Keyword-Based Image Moderation:
     Equipment photos (up to 5 per tool) and user avatars support URLs or Base64
     data URLs stored in SQLite/localStorage, and automated image moderation
     (packages/shared/src/image-moderation.ts) performs MIME-type checks,
     SVG XSS/script sanitization, and filename/payload keyword filtering rather
     than external cloud vision AI inference (ensuring offline autonomy).

  5. Static Frontend Route Bundling:
     Page components in frontend/src/app/routes.tsx are imported statically
     rather than code-split with React.lazy(), resulting in a single minified
     frontend bundle (~516 kB) that loads in <200 ms on local LANs.

5. FUNCTIONAL AND NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

5.1 Functional Requirements (FR)
--------------------------------
  FR-1  Resident Registration & Eligibility Verification:
        Users can register as Borrowers by providing full name, email, password
        (min 8 chars), phone, birth_date (YYYY-MM-DD), Basco barangay, province
        (default 'Batanes'), and residential address. The system rejects
        registrants under 18 years old both in Zod validation and AuthService.

  FR-2  Authentication, Sessions & Brute-Force Protection:
        Users log in via email and password. Sessions issue a 32-byte random
        token stored in SQLite (sessions) for 7 days and delivered via HTTP-only
        cookie (mcteb_session) and JSON bearer token. Logout deletes the session
        immediately. Five consecutive failed login attempts lock the account for
        15 minutes.

  FR-3  Equipment Catalog Discovery & Inventory Management:
        Guests and authenticated users can search, filter (by category,
        availability, condition, owner), and sort catalog tools, and inspect
        multi-image galleries (up to 5 photos), serial numbers, quantities, and
        specifications. Municipal Staff and Administrators can create, edit,
        verify serial number uniqueness, archive, and delete tools. Borrowers
        are strictly blocked (403 Forbidden) from modifying catalog inventory.

  FR-4  Advance Reservation Lifecycle:
        Borrowers submit reservation requests specifying tool, quantity, pickup
        and return dates, and purpose. The backend checks tool status (blocking
        Archived/Damaged tools) and overlapping date inventory capacity. Staff
        review Pending requests (Approve or Reject with notes), while borrowers
        or staff can cancel Pending/Approved bookings.

  FR-5  Physical Handover Desk & Return Inspection:
        At the physical desk, Municipal Staff or Administrators release tools
        (with optional reservation linkage), atomically decrementing
        available_quantity, creating a Borrowed rental record, and marking the
        originating reservation Completed. Upon return, staff record returned
        condition and inspection notes, incrementing available_quantity and
        flagging damaged/needs-repair tools to 'Maintenance' availability.

  FR-6  Tool Borrowing Receipt Generation & Export:
        Borrowers and staff can open, edit, print, and download an 8-section
        Tool Borrowing Receipt Form pre-filled from rental or approved
        reservation records (Organization Info, Borrower Info, Tool Info,
        Borrowing Schedule, Return Details, Status Flags, Warning/Penalty Terms,
        and Approval Signatures) as an A4 PDF soft copy or Word (.doc) file.

  FR-7  Equipment Maintenance & Repair Board:
        Staff and Administrators can file maintenance defect reports with
        severity levels (Minor, Moderate, Severe, Critical). Severe/Critical
        reports automatically switch the tool to Maintenance / Needs Repair.
        Updating a ticket to Completed with return_to_service=true restores the
        tool to Available / Good.

  FR-8  Community Feedback & Privacy-Preserving Moderation:
        Borrowers submit 3-axis ratings (overall_rating, tool_condition_rating,
        service_rating, 1-5) and comments. The public endpoint masks reviewer
        surnames (e.g., "Ana R."). Administrators can moderate public visibility
        (is_public).

  FR-9  In-App Notifications:
        The system dispatches alerts to users upon registration, reservation
        submission/approval/rejection/cancellation, rental release, return
        inspection, and role/status updates, with unread counters and deep
        links.

  FR-10 Administrative Perimeter & Audit Trail:
        Administrators access /api/v1/admin/overview, /api/v1/admin/health, user
        management (role/status changes with self-suspension and sole-admin
        demotion safeguards), and read-only security audit logs (audit_logs).

5.2 Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)
------------------------------------
  NFR-1 Reliability & Data Atomicity:
        All multi-table mutations execute within BEGIN IMMEDIATE ... COMMIT /
        ROLLBACK atomic wrappers (db.atomic()). SQLite runs in WAL mode with
        foreign_keys=ON, synchronous=NORMAL, and busy_timeout=5000.

  NFR-2 Security & Access Control:
        Passwords use node:crypto scrypt with 16-byte random salts and 64-byte
        keys verified via crypto.timingSafeEqual. All SQL queries use
        parameterized prepared statements. Helmet CSP, CORS origin allowlisting,
        IP rate limiting, and Zod payload validation protect all endpoints.

  NFR-3 Maintainability & Type Safety:
        100% TypeScript (.ts/.tsx) application code compiled under strict flags
        (strict: true, noImplicitAny: true, strictNullChecks: true). Single
        source of truth for DTOs and schemas in @mcteb/shared.

  NFR-4 Portability & Zero Native Build Overhead:
        Uses Node.js >= 20 built-in node:sqlite (DatabaseSync), requiring no
        external database service or C++ compiler across Windows 10/11, macOS,
        and Ubuntu Linux.

  NFR-5 Usability & Responsiveness:
        Responsive UI built with Tailwind CSS v3.4 supporting desktop sidebar
        and mobile drawer layouts, dark/light theme persistence, accessible form
        labels, and clear feedback states (Alert, Badge, Skeleton, EmptyState).

7. SYSTEM DESIGN AND COMPONENT DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

7.1 Shared Contract Package (packages/shared — @mcteb/shared)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - src/enums.ts & src/enums/index.ts:
    Defines canonical Zod enums and TypeScript types:
    * UserRole: 'Administrator' | 'Municipal Staff' | 'Borrower'
    * UserStatus: 'Active' | 'Pending Verification' | 'Suspended'
    * ToolAvailabilityStatus: 'Available' | 'Reserved' | 'Borrowed' |
      'Returned' | 'Maintenance' | 'Damaged' | 'Archived'
    * ToolConditionStatus: 'Excellent' | 'Good' | 'Fair' | 'Needs Repair' |
      'Under Maintenance'
    * ReservationStatus: 'Pending' | 'Approved' | 'Rejected' | 'Cancelled' |
      'Completed'
    * RentalStatus: 'Pending' | 'Reserved' | 'Borrowed' | 'Returned' |
      'Completed'
    * TOOL_CATEGORIES: Agriculture, Fishing, Carpentry, Construction,
      Electrical, Power Equipment, Emergency Response
    * MaintenanceSeverity: 'Minor' | 'Moderate' | 'Severe' | 'Critical'
    * MaintenanceStatus: 'Reported' | 'Scheduled' | 'In Progress' |
      'Completed' | 'Decommissioned'
    * Barangay: Chanarian, Ihubok II (Kaychanarianan), Ihubok I (Kayvaluganan),
      Kayhuvokan, San Antonio, San Joaquin
  - src/permissions.ts:
    Authoritative RBAC permission matrix (Permissions) mapping 19 granular
    actions (e.g., CATALOG_VIEW, RESERVATION_CREATE, RENTAL_RELEASE,
    TOOL_MANAGE, USER_ROLE_UPDATE) to allowed roles via hasPermission().
  - src/schemas/:
    Zod schemas for audit, feedback, maintenance, notification, rental,
    reservation, tool, and user validation (including RegisterUserSchema 18+
    age refinement and 5-image tool upload cap).
  - src/dates.ts:
    Manila timezone (Asia/Manila) date parsing and formatting helpers.
  - src/image-moderation.ts:
    Multi-image validator (MAX_TOOL_IMAGES = 5) enforcing valid image URLs/data
    URLs, blocking embedded <script>/XSS payloads in SVGs, and rejecting
    filenames/content matching prohibited categories (Explicit/adult, Violent/
    graphic, Hate symbols, Illegal/inappropriate materials).

7.2 Backend Application (backend — @mcteb/backend)
--------------------------------------------------
  - Database Layer (backend/db/):
    * client.ts: Singleton wrapper around Node's DatabaseSync opening
      Database/app.db with WAL, foreign_keys=ON, busy_timeout=5000, and
      synchronous=NORMAL. Exposes db.atomic(fn) executing BEGIN IMMEDIATE /
      COMMIT / ROLLBACK.
    * schema.sql: Canonical DDL for 8 core domain tables + 16 performance
      indexes (plus sessions table ensured by migrate.ts & session.repository.ts).
    * init.ts, migrate.ts: Idempotent database initialization and schema
      evolution (including automatic removal of legacy fee/payment columns and
      backfilling user age from birth_date).
    * seed/ (seed.ts, reset-demo.ts, validate-fixtures.ts, fixtures/index.ts):
      Idempotent seeding of 4 demo accounts (a0000000-... prefixes) and 7
      catalog tools (b0000000-... prefixes), guarded against accidental runs
      when NODE_ENV=production unless ALLOW_DEMO_SEED=true.
  - Configuration & Utilities (backend/src/config/, backend/src/utils/):
    * env.ts: Zod-validated environment loader (NODE_ENV, PORT, DATABASE_PATH,
      CORS_ORIGINS, SESSION_SECRET, LOG_LEVEL, ALLOW_DEMO_SEED) with automatic
      path resolution for Database/app.db.
    * crypto.ts: scrypt password hashing/verification, timingSafeEqual, 32-byte
      session token generation, and UUID v4 generation.
    * errors.ts: AppError hierarchy (400 badRequest, 401 unauthorized, 403
      forbidden, 404 notFound, 409 conflict, 500 internal).
    * logger.ts: Structured Pino logger instance.
  - Middleware (backend/src/middleware/):
    * auth.ts: extractAuthToken (cookie mcteb_session or Bearer header),
      authenticate (validates active session, lockout_until, and Suspended
      status), requireAuth, requireRole, and requirePermission.
    * rateLimit.ts: globalRateLimiter (300 req / 15 min) and authRateLimiter
      (10 req / 15 min).
    * validate.ts: Generic Zod request validation middleware factory.
    * errorHandler.ts: Centralized translation of AppError, ZodError, and
      unexpected errors into standardized JSON responses.
  - Domain Modules (src/routes/, src/controllers/, src/services/,
    src/repositories/):
    * Auth (auth): Registration, login, logout, session lookup (/me).
    * Users (users): User listing, profile updates, status/role updates.
    * Tools (tools): Catalog filtering, serial verification, tool CRUD, archival.
    * Reservations (reservations): Advance booking creation, date overlap stock
      check, staff review (Approve/Reject), borrower/staff cancellation.
    * Rentals (rentals): Handover release, return inspection, atomic stock
      adjustments, maintenance auto-flagging on damaged returns.
    * Maintenance (maintenance): Defect reporting, repair workflow, return to
      service restoration.
    * Feedback (feedback): 3-axis ratings, surname-masked public reviews,
      admin visibility moderation.
    * Notifications (notifications): User alert feed, mark read / mark all read.
    * Audit (audit): Immutable security and action log recording and querying.
  - Administrative Perimeter (backend/src/admin/):
    * admin.routes.ts, admin.controller.ts, admin.service.ts: Dedicated
      Administrator-only route subtree (/api/v1/admin/*) serving system overview
      counts, live SQLite health metrics (database path, size bytes, table
      count, uptime, Node version), user management, and audit access.

9. SYSTEM INTEGRATION AND COMMUNICATION EXPLANATION
================================================================================

9.1 Shared Contract Integration (@mcteb/shared)
-----------------------------------------------
  Both frontend and backend import validation schemas, domain enums, permission
  constants, and TypeScript interfaces directly from @mcteb/shared. When a form
  is submitted on the frontend (e.g., RegisterUserSchema or CreateToolSchema),
  the exact same Zod validation rules and TypeScript contract execute on the
  backend controller, eliminating schema drift between client and server.

9.2 Client-Server HTTP/JSON Communication
-----------------------------------------
  - Development Proxy: Vite dev server on http://localhost:5173 proxies all
    /api/* requests to http://localhost:3000 (frontend/vite.config.ts),
    eliminating CORS preflight friction during local development.
  - API Client (frontend/src/lib/api.ts): All client requests pass through
    apiRequest<T>() using native fetch() with Content-Type: application/json,
    credentials: 'include' (sending the mcteb_session HTTP-only cookie), and
    Authorization: Bearer <token> read from localStorage ('mcteb_token').
  - Server State Caching: TanStack Query (@tanstack/react-query v5) caches
    REST responses, manages loading/error states, and invalidates queries after
    mutations.

9.3 Core End-to-End Transactional Workflows
-------------------------------------------
  1. Authentication & Session Establishment:
     User submits credentials -> authRateLimiter checks IP quota (10 req / 15m)
     -> AuthController.login parses LoginSchema -> AuthService.login checks
     lockout_until -> crypto.verifyPassword runs scrypt + timingSafeEqual ->
     on failure, increments failed_login_attempts (locks for 15 min at 5); on
     success, resets counter, creates 7-day session in SQLite, records
     USER_LOGIN in audit_logs, sets mcteb_session HTTP-only cookie, and returns
     { user, token }.

  2. Advance Reservation Submission & Staff Review:
     Borrower submits POST /api/v1/reservations -> ReservationsService validates
     tool availability status and checks overlapping approved/pending quantity
     against total_quantity -> inside db.atomic(), inserts Pending reservation,
     writes RESERVATION_CREATED audit log, notifies borrower, and dispatches
     alerts to all Municipal Staff and Administrators. When staff executes
     PATCH /api/v1/reservations/:id/review, an atomic transaction updates status
     (Approved/Rejected), records audit log, and notifies the borrower.

  3. Physical Handover Release & Return Inspection:
     - Handover (POST /api/v1/rentals/release): Inside db.atomic(), verifies
       available_quantity >= quantity, creates Borrowed rental record,
       decrements tools.available_quantity, marks linked reservation Completed,
       writes RENTAL_RELEASED audit log, and notifies the borrower.
     - Return (POST /api/v1/rentals/:id/return): Inside db.atomic(), records
       returned_condition and inspection_notes, sets rental to Returned,
       increments tools.available_quantity, and if returned_condition is
       'Needs Repair' or 'Under Maintenance', updates tool availability_status to
       'Maintenance' and condition_status accordingly, logs RENTAL_RETURNED, and
       notifies the borrower.

11. SECURITY IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

  1. Password Cryptography & Timing-Attack Resistance (backend/src/utils/crypto.ts):
     - Hashes passwords using Node's native scrypt KDF with a 16-byte random
       hex salt and 64-byte derived key, formatted as:
       scrypt$<salt_hex>$<hash_hex>.
     - Verifies credentials using crypto.timingSafeEqual() after validating
       buffer length equality, preventing microsecond timing side-channel leaks.

  2. Brute-Force & Rate-Limiting Controls:
     - authRateLimiter restricts /auth/register and /auth/login to 10 requests
       per 15-minute window per IP; globalRateLimiter caps general API traffic at
       300 requests per 15-minute window.
     - AuthRepository.incrementFailedAttempts tracks consecutive failed logins
       and locks the account (lockout_until = +15 minutes) on the 5th failure.
       Subsequent login or authenticated requests while locked or Suspended are
       rejected with 403 Forbidden. Successful login resets the failure counter.

  3. Session Management & Token Revocation (backend/src/repositories/session.repository.ts):
     - Generates 32-byte (256-bit) random hex tokens stored server-side in the
       SQLite sessions table with a 7-day TTL.
     - Supports dual transport: HTTP-only cookie (mcteb_session; httpOnly: true,
       sameSite: 'lax', secure: true in production) and Authorization: Bearer.
     - Logout immediately deletes the token row from SQLite and clears the
       cookie.

  4. Server-Enforced RBAC & IDOR Protection:
     - Frontend ProtectedRoute serves strictly as a UX safeguard; all security
       boundaries are enforced server-side via requireAuth, requireRole(), and
       requirePermission().
     - /api/v1/admin/* mounts requireAuth + requireRole('Administrator') globally
       on the router perimeter.
     - Service-layer IDOR checks ensure Borrowers can only view/cancel their own
       reservations, rentals, and profiles (req.user.id), and are explicitly
       blocked (403 Forbidden) from creating, editing, archiving, or deleting
       catalog tools.

  5. Administrative Deadlock & Self-Lockout Safeguards (users.service.ts):
     - Administrators cannot suspend their own active account.
     - The system counts remaining active Administrators and blocks suspending
       or demoting the sole remaining active Administrator.

  6. SQL Injection, XSS, & Content Poisoning Defenses:
     - 100% of SQLite queries execute through parameterized statements
       (db.prepare('... ?')).
     - Dual-layer validation pairs shared Zod schemas (stripping/rejecting
       invalid fields) with SQLite CHECK constraints and PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON.
     - Helmet configures strict Content Security Policy (defaultSrc 'self',
       scriptSrc 'self') and disables X-Powered-By.
     - Image moderation (packages/shared/src/image-moderation.ts) rejects SVGs
       containing <script>, javascript:, or inline event handlers (onload=,
       onerror=), enforces the 5-image cap, and filters prohibited content.

  7. Privacy Protection & Immutable Audit Logging:
     - Public community feedback (/api/v1/feedback/public) truncates reviewer
       surnames (e.g., "Ana R.") and excludes phone numbers and addresses.
     - Security-sensitive events (USER_REGISTERED, USER_LOGIN,
       USER_STATUS_UPDATED, USER_ROLE_UPDATED, RESERVATION_*, RENTAL_*,
       MAINTENANCE_*, FEEDBACK_*) are persisted in audit_logs with actor ID,
       target entity, details, and client IP address.

14. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

Conclusion:
  The Municipal Community Tool and Equipment Bank (MCTEB) delivers a reliable,
  secure, and maintainable digital platform tailored to the operational realities
  of Basco, Batanes. By combining an end-to-end TypeScript monorepo
  (@mcteb/shared, @mcteb/backend, @mcteb/frontend) with Node.js's built-in
  node:sqlite engine in WAL mode, MCTEB eliminates external database setup
  friction and sustains full functionality even during island WAN outages.
  Strict 4-tier backend separation of concerns, atomic multi-row database
  operations, scrypt + timing-safe authentication, server-enforced RBAC with
  IDOR and admin-deadlock protections, standardized A4 PDF Tool Borrowing
  Receipts, and 28 passing automated tests across all workspace packages ensure
  both civic accountability and long-term technical maintainability.

Recommendations for Future Phases & Operational Hardening:
  1. Phase 2 — Hardware & Desk Automation:
     - Add printable QR/barcode asset tags linked to tool serial numbers so desk
       staff can scan equipment with a webcam or USB barcode scanner during
       handover and return inspection.
     - Integrate an HTML5 canvas digital signature pad into
       ToolBorrowingReceiptForm.tsx for touch-screen signing at the municipal
       rental counter.

  2. Phase 3 — Cellular SMS Gateway Integration:
     - Integrate a Philippine SMS gateway (e.g., Semaphore API) alongside the
       existing in-app NotificationsService to dispatch SMS reminders for
       approved pickups, overdue returns, and typhoon emergency equipment
       availability to residents in low-connectivity barangays.

  3. Phase 4 — PWA Offline Sync & Inter-Island Scaling:
     - Implement Service Worker caching and IndexedDB offline queueing so desk
       tablets in remote barangays or neighboring islands (Sabtang, Itbayat)
       can record handovers offline and auto-sync when connectivity returns.
     - If expanded into a multi-municipality provincial system, migrate the
       repository layer (backend/src/repositories/) from SQLite to PostgreSQL;
       the strict 4-tier architecture preserves service business logic intact.

  4. Production Operations & Performance Optimization:
     - Deploy behind Nginx or Caddy with TLS/HTTPS enabled so NODE_ENV=production
       enforces the `secure` flag on `mcteb_session` cookies.
     - Schedule automated cron/Task Scheduler execution of `pnpm run db:backup`
       and configure log rotation for Pino JSON logs.
     - Refactor static page imports in frontend/src/app/routes.tsx to use
       React.lazy() and <Suspense> to eliminate the >500 kB Vite chunk warning,
       and run `pnpm run format` to maintain 100% Prettier consistency.
